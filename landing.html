GOOD QUESTIONS

Everything you need
to know first.

Does Ledgerlane replace our accounting system?+

No. Ledgerlane is the assurance layer above it. It connects your financial sources, turns repeatable close work into controlled workflows, and preserves the evidence around every decision.

Can we start with one close process?+

Yes. Most teams begin with a high-friction reconciliation, accrual, or evidence collection control, then expand once the operating rhythm is established.

How does the AI stay grounded?+

Lane answers against the financial records and evidence attached to your workspace. It shows the supporting sources and escalates material decisions to the people who own them.

Do you support enterprise security requirements?+

Scale includes SSO, custom retention, and implementation support. Contact us to map your control and procurement requirements.
